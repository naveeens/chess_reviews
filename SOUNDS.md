# Sounds

The move, capture, game-end, puzzle-solved and puzzle-failed sounds in `app/src/main/res/raw/` (`snd_*.mp3`) are
the **Standard** set from Lichess's mobile app:
https://github.com/lichess-org/mobile/tree/main/assets/sounds/standard

Lichess publishes its code and sounds under the GPL / AGPL (see https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md).
The app is published under the GPL v3 (see LICENSE), which is compatible with these files. Lichess is credited in
Settings > About > Open-source licences.
