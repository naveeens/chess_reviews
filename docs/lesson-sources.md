# Lesson sources

The lesson comments are written in our own words. Ideas, plans and warnings were taken from the references below, and every
line was checked against Stockfish 17.1 (depth 20). The evaluations in the PGNs come from that engine, not from the sources.
Where a source and the engine disagreed, the engine won.

- Wikipedia: Italian Game, Scotch Game, Vienna Game, Three Knights Game, Bishop's Opening, Colle System, Réti Opening,
  Caro–Kann Defence, Queen's Gambit.
- Chessable blog: Colle System (castle first, take on c5 before pushing e4); The Réti Opening (plans for White and Black).
- chess.com blogs on the Réti (fianchetto, pressure on d5 from the flank).

Not covered by a strong source, so read with care: the notes on the Colle-Zukertort and on the Greek gift, which sources
describe as debated, and the short Chigorin line in the Colle lesson.
